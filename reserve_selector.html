V21 · Interactive reserve selector

How much should
the battery hold back?

A six-band bid can reserve stored energy for rare high-price intervals. The reserve is an inventory floor: if the battery starts at or above R MWh, ordinary-price discharge cannot take it below R.

These controls select actual saved October 2026 trajectories at five reserve levels. The page does not retrain a model, re-solve a bid, or submit anything to a market.

03 / What the floor guarantees

A conditional
inventory guarantee.

(Δt / ηd) · Σordinary bands qd,b ≤ max(0, Et − R)

If the battery enters an interval with at least R MWh, every clearing ordinary-price band is covered by this single deterministic row, no spike-price band clears, and charging is nonnegative, then the next stored energy is at least R.

What it does not promise

It does not guarantee that a high-price band has positive quantity, that a spike is captured, or that the reserve is economically optimal. It protects inventory only. The V21 study separates that protection from the learned and hand-designed bid quantities that decide whether the reserve is used.

Across R=0/2/4/6/8, the learned critic's stress premium over a hand-designed value falls from +12.7k AUD to +2.0k AUD: the reserve substitutes for learned inventory protection. Reserve-level sweep.
